Author: Claude Code (lead). Reviewer: Codex (`adversarial-review`, read-only, 2026-10-26). Tier: T2 (two review rounds at most; fixes after round 2 go on the D-016 list for the B1 gate). The owner merges.

Before the review the lead found and fixed, test-first: WNTR stores `demand_model = "PDD"` as `"PDA"` (the canonical value is set now); WNTR 1.5 leaves `options.energy.global_efficiency` unset, which `pump_power` divides by (the test sets it); `WNTRSimulator` lets a filling tank pass its maximum by one step's inflow (bounded at 1 mm and written down); the procedural network's PRVs could not regulate and stopped the simulator (fixed by T-B1-011b, #48).
