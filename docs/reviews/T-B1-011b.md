# Review log: T-B1-011b, PRVs that can regulate

Author: Codex (fix card written by the lead). Reviewer: Claude Code (lead), by running and mutating it, 2026-10-26. Tier: T1.

**Why the card exists.** While building the hydraulic engine (T-B1-016), the lead found that the two procedural PRVs sat on random street pipes inside the meshed grid: EPANET reported both closed for a whole 48-hour run (downstream about 60 m against a 25 m setting), and WNTR's `WNTRSimulator` with pressure-driven demand did not converge at time 0. With the PRVs replaced by open pipes, or given a setting they can hold, both simulators ran and agreed within 0.005 m.

**What the lead checked.**
- The rule as specified: each PRV is the sole inlet of a named district (`residential_outer`, `green`); the zone holds no source, station discharge or tank; the static margin rule; orientation (start on the source side). Topology holds for seeds 1 to 40 (lead script), not only the 1 to 5 of the tests.
- Hydraulics (Codex's tests, rerun): seed 1, both PRVs active in 100% of 289 steps at 25.0 m mean downstream pressure; `WNTRSimulator` converges for 24 hours.
- Junction ids and base demands unchanged (the demand model's golden hashes depend on them); only the two zero-demand split junctions moved.

| Severity | Finding | Decision | Fixed in |
|---|---|---|---|
| S3 | `isolate_prv_zones` adds unused street candidates to keep the pipe target without checking that they stay inside a zone; a crossing candidate would bypass the PRV. No seed from 1 to 40 produces one. | Accepted as a guard: candidates that cross a zone boundary are skipped. Its mutation is equivalent for the packaged config. | lead commit `c3602cf` |
| S3 | The government-exclusion test matched a pattern that the source-site error also matches (the government district holds the source), so removing the exclusion left the tests green (lead mutation). | Accepted: the test matches the exact message. | lead commit `c3602cf` |

Mutation run: 9 mutations of the new rules; 8 caught after the test fix; the survivor is the equivalent guard above.
