# Handoff: T-B1-016, hydraulic engine (WNTR, pressure-driven demand)

Author: Claude Code (lead). Reviewer: Codex (adversarial, two rounds at most). Tier 2: the owner merges.

## What it does
`ug_water.solver.simulate(network, demand, options=None) -> HydraulicResult` turns a `Network` and a demand series (anything with `timebase`, sorted `junction_ids` and a `(ticks, junctions)` array in m3/s; the demand model's `DemandSeries` fits) into the twin's truth:
- `build_model` makes the WNTR model: one pattern per junction (base = mean of the series), duty pumps open and standby closed, isolation valves as open TCVs with loss 0, PRVs at their setting, one 5-minute step for hydraulics, patterns and reports, pressure-driven demand (`PDA`) with the settings of `solver/data/hydraulics_v1.json` (`ASSUMPTION`), Hazen-Williams head loss.
- `simulate` runs `WNTRSimulator` (default; its leak model is the one T-B1-017 uses) or `EpanetSimulator` and returns a `TruthSeries` (`SIMULATION`) with channels `"<quantity>:<element>"`: junction `pressure`, `head`, `demand` (delivered); tank `level`, `head`; link `flowrate`; pipe `velocity`; pump `power` (kW), plus `energy_kwh` per pump.
- Fail closed: non-convergence, non-finite results, a results axis that is not the series axis, reverse pump flow and a demand series that does not fit are `HydraulicsError`.

## Evidence
- 48 h at 5 min on the procedural network (260 junctions): 5.7 s on the lead's laptop (Spec 4.8 budget 60 s), with the PRV fix of T-B1-011b (#48).
- PDD: the delivered share matches `((p - p_min)/(p_req - p_min)) ** 0.5` at 3, 5 and 12 m within 0.1%; full delivery at 40 m.
- Energy: WNTR's `pump_power` (its global efficiency set to the pump's 75%, because WNTR 1.5 leaves it unset) agrees within 1%; a hand calculation agrees to rounding.
- WNTR and EPANET agree on the loop within 0.05 m and 1% of pump flow.
- 36 tests; 15 mutations of the engine and builder, 14 caught (the survivor clamps flows below 1e-9 m3/s to zero, equivalent in practice).
- Approximations written down: `docs/approximations.md` (engine, PDD, time step, tanks overshooting by up to one step's inflow in WNTRSimulator, pump power and energy, failures, performance).
