# Handoff: D-03 hydraulic faults for the demo slice (lead, Tier 2)

`ug_water/solver/faults.py`: `plan_faults` (checks faults against the network and horizon), `apply_faults` (WNTR changes), `add_fire_flows` (demand). The adapter no longer refuses faults.

| Fault | How (WNTR 1.5.0, introspected) |
|---|---|
| leak sudden, burst | `split_pipe` at the midpoint, `Junction.add_leak(area, Cd 0.75, start_time)` at the onset tick |
| leak gradual | `leak_area` has no setter: 4 equal leaks along the pipe starting at k x ramp/4 (staircase, ASSUMPTION) |
| pump outage | exact time controls (`Pump.add_outage`'s rule acts 2 steps late); the pump's level controls are gated outside the window (`AndCondition`/`OrCondition`, now declared, manifest regenerated); the station's free standby takes over (opened at start, follows the duty's level controls, closed at end) |
| fire flow | extra demand at the node in its window |

Onset tick = ceil(start_hour x 3600 / step); a fault at or after the last tick is refused; unknown elements, two leaks on one pipe, sensor faults ("not in the demo slice") and a leak with EPANET are refused.
Truth: hidden channel `leak:<pipe>` (m3/s) and one `TruthEvent` per fault targeting the bare element id (the store tags that element's channels).

## Measured (SIMULATION, procedural seed 1, presets, 48 h)
leak_sudden 6 cm2: 10.8 L/s from 30 h. leak_gradual_medium: 2.2 -> 8.7 L/s. burst_main 50 cm2: 112 L/s. pump_outage_standby: duty_0 0 L/s from 20 h to 26 h, standby_0 114 L/s in the window. Min pressure 24.5 m in all. Peak memory about 1 GB per run.

## Not done
- After an outage a duty pump with a level control waits for its control (it does not restart at once).
- Sensor faults stay in the measurement what-ifs (D-04); background leakage, valve state, power loss, pump degradation are not in the slice.
