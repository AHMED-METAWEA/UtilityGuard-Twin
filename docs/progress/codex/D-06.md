## API and evidence choices for the lead

- Public exports: `Rate`, `ExposureRates`, `Option`, `DEFAULT_OPTIONS`, `CostEngine`, `compare`.
- `CostEngine(rates, assumptions, *, seed, provenance, decision_id, n_samples=1000, options=None)` provides `cost_lines(option)` and copies of `total_samples(option)`, `regret_samples(option)` and `assumption_samples`. `option` may be the frozen Option or its ID.
- `compare` uses the same arguments plus `incident_id`. The `expected_cost`, `worst_case` and `expected_regret` fields are **evidence ID strings**, resolving in the top-level `evidence` array. The card supplies field names but not these value types; this choice was surfaced to the owner while implementing and has not received confirmation.
- Each wait option has a `net_benefit` evidence record; it is discoverable by `explanation_template="net_benefit.v1"`, with its sign retained. The default fix_now/wait_24h pair has a `break_even_lost_water_rate` record, linked from `break_even`.
- Repair and crew quantities are one `count` each, meaning one repair and one visit. These counts and the option delay have ASSUMPTION evidence records. Non-isolated physical quantity inputs retain the supplied rate label/ref.
- Isolation's unserved quantity combines **two** rates. Its money line references an additional physical `unserved_quantity` evidence record, which names both rate refs and both durations. This preserves exactly one quantity and one price on the money line. The physical quantity is MODEL if either rate is MODEL, otherwise SIMULATION. This composite ref is an explicit interpretation of the card's singular quantity ref; the lead should check it with D-01.
- Quantity inputs expose sampled p50, prices and durations expose assumption modes as requested. Their displayed point values need not multiply to the sampled mean; costs always use the full paired samples. The seed, sample count, assumption set and formula identify the calculation.
- Break-even sums the other four lines directly for F (avoids cancellation from subtracting large lost-water costs). It uses E[X * price], not E[X] * E[price]. Inputs reference both options' money lines.

## Doubts / limits

- Acceptance item 3 says a different seed changes "the uncertainty only", while the explicit formulas require expected costs to be sample means. These requirements cannot both hold literally: changing a seed generally changes sample means and p50 quantity inputs as well as percentiles, and may change rankings. Implementation follows the explicit sampling formulas; IDs, formulas, labels and source refs remain fixed. This was surfaced to the owner while implementing and remains a lead review point.
- The comparison contract is being drafted separately; no contract or generated type changes were attempted here. UI rendering of the caveat/banner/Fidelity/source and action approval belongs to downstream consumers.
- Same inputs and seed reproduce outputs within the recorded NumPy version; NumPy does not promise streams across versions, as documented by SeedStreams.
- No claims of real network probabilities, field validation or actual ACUD savings. Costs and net benefits are model-estimated, conditional on assumptions.
