### Decisions, doubts and review focus

- Calibration follows the card literally: all non-missing residuals from ticks 0–287, including readings marked bad, contribute to MAD. Missing/bad readings never update the post-calibration statistic or persistence counter. The calibration window is not extended for missing ticks. No residuals means the 2 × quantum floor.
- Persistence counts updated ticks. Missing/bad gaps hold the counter; any updated value with max statistic <= h resets it. An alarm is retained at its first occurrence. A low direction wins when the negative statistic exceeds h at the alarm tick, per the card.
- The card leaves the exact result classes and contributor filtering implicit. Leak contributors are the qualifying pressure-low/flow-high alarms within six ticks of the first alarm; other hypotheses retain all alarming sensors. Support/rules use that contributor set. Lead should confirm this interpretation. Sensor-fault requires exactly one alarm across the whole result, with Q04/Q08 specifically at that alarm tick; it cannot override a multi-sensor case.
- A tank estimate requires the latest tick to have a level reading. A missing latest value yields INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE rather than silently treating an older level as current. Rising/flat levels and fewer than four non-null window points also produce no estimate. Unavailable evidence inputs are omitted, with a reason on the record.
- Independently repeated WNTR solves can differ around 1e-14. The mapping test checks exact values against one solver result; independent runs use the existing `round_for_hash` precision (six decimals), matching the solver's established determinism convention. No rounding was added to production expectations.
- Clean integration fixtures drive both fault-free hydraulics and expectations with the same constructed MODEL demand. They verify the telemetry/quality/detector path, not accuracy against independent real demand or field data.
- Input evidence labels are specified for simulated levels only. The tank evidence helper explicitly documents SENSOR_SIM input; an observed-data evidence builder is outside this card.
- No localization, equipment actions, UI integration, tuning claims or field-validation claims are included.
